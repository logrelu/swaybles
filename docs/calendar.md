# 90-day charm calendar (Day 1 = 1 Oct, Day 90 = 29 Dec, finale on NYE)

About three new charms a week. Use the next unused idea in the current month unless the audience
voted for something better. Mark each one when shipped. No brand characters, memes or real people.
Joke text goes on a blank surface drawn by the app, never in the image.
